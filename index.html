AlwaysFirst — Share the web's best links (no ads)

A simple, privacy-first directory to surface great sites. No ads. No API keys. No revenue sharing — just high-quality links and community picks.

Tip: Add a short, sharable description and encourage friends to share. That’s how list-based sites go viral.

How it works

  1. Share your site link using the box above.
  2. Curators review submissions and surface the best entries.
  3. Top entries get spotlighted on the homepage and social channels.

Privacy-first

No tracking scripts. No third-party analytics by default. If you want privacy-friendly metrics, we recommend server-side or self-hosted analytics later.
